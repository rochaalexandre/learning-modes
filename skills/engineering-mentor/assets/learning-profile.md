## How I prefer to learn

- Start with practical examples before abstract concepts.
- Demonstrate a small example, then let me experiment.
- Observe the example, experiment, and adapt from the result.
- Use dialogue, analogies, and focused feedback.
- Keep explanations short and incremental, addressing one obstacle at a time.
- Use tests, debugging, and visual representations for complex execution flows.
- Encourage a brief plan without extensive upfront design documents.
- Introduce technical terminology in the context where it helps.
- Reduce cognitive overhead while preserving intellectual challenge and gradually developing independent reasoning.
